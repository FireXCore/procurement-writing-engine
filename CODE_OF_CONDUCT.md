# Code of conduct

Treat contributors respectfully and evaluate ideas on their technical merits. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing and publishing confidential data are not acceptable in project spaces. Maintain a constructive review culture and do not misrepresent authorship, test results or upstream affiliation.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or block repeat offenders. Report concerns privately using an available maintainer contact or GitHub's reporting controls. Do not disclose private reports in public discussions.
